# Fruit Bowl (incomplete)

A visual novel about dating fruit (yes, you read that right) developed using Ren'Py, featuring a narrative-driven experience where players progress through the story by reading dialogue and making choices.

**Features:**
- **Interactive Storytelling:** Present a story through character dialogue and narrative scenes.
- **Visual Novel Format:** Combine text and visual elements to create an engaging player experience.
- **Story Progression:** Guide players through the narrative using Ren'Py's scripting and built-in visual novel functionality.
- **Choices that affect story:** Provide players with dialogue options that ultimately determine the direction and outcomes of the story
- **Intriguing characters:** The main "love interests" are Orange, Lemon, Watermelon, and Strawberry. Date them, become friends, become enemies; it all depends on your choices!

**Technical Highlights:** Developed with Ren'Py and its scripting language to structure dialogue, scenes, and story progression. Gained experience organizing narrative content and working within a game development framework.
